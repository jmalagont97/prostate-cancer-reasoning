### [2026-08-24] - Formulation and Execution of Subtask 1.3 Coalesced SHAP Space (`exp_8`)
- **Principal Investigator Proposal (User):** Replicate the multivariate tree attribution approach of historical `exp_22` using the optimal leak-free tabular branch from Task 1.1 (`exp_4`). Proposed handling collinearity by merging/coalescing SHAP values for correlated features and evaluating balanced models on full SHAP vectors across the 30-split MCCV protocol in `tmux:0`.
- **Co-Investigator Analysis (AI):** Analyzed feature collinearity ($PSA \leftrightarrow PSAD$, $PIRADS \leftrightarrow csPCa$, $Vol \leftrightarrow PSAD$) against ground-truth urological weights, proving that rigid label tying ($\hat{y}_j = \hat{y}_k$) collapses minority classes in clinical practice (e.g. $csPCa$ is ignored in $67\%$ of cases while $PIRADS$ is critical in $98.9\%$). Proposed *Coalesced Feature Augmentation* ($\boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\text{aug}} \in \mathbb{R}^{13}$), appending coalition energy sums as inputs while training balanced Random Forests per clinical variable.
- **Consensus:** Executed `exp_8` across all 30 MCCV folds. Condition $C_4$ (Coalesced-Balanced-Forest) achieved $\overline{\text{Macro-F1}} = 0.2523 \pm 0.0299$, $S_{\text{weights}} = 0.8050 \pm 0.0219$, $\text{Decisive Factors Set-F1} = 0.6411 \pm 0.0375$, and positive rank correlation across primary drivers ($\rho(\text{PSA}) = +0.1314$, $\rho(\text{PIRADS}) = +0.2169$, $\rho(\text{DRE}) = +0.2053$). Confirmed $C_4$ as the Subtask 1.3 benchmark, outperforming raw trees ($p = 0.000608$).

### [2026-08-24] - Formulation and Execution of Joint Feature-Attribution Fusion 23D (`exp_9`)
- **Principal Investigator Proposal (User):** Noted that pure SHAP models remain close to chance ($\text{Macro-F1} \approx 0.25$) due to polarity ambiguity and proposed appending raw clinical feature values $\mathbf{x}$ to the coalesced SHAP vectors for multivariate relevance modeling.
- **Co-Investigator Analysis (AI):** Diagnosed that pure Shapley attributions $|\phi_j|$ quantify boundary sensitivity but discard the patient's absolute phenotypic baseline ($PSA=0.5$ vs $PSA=50.0$), whereas direct clinical variables ($DRE$, $Bx$, $PIRADS$) carry strong independent direct correlations with specialist weights ($x_{\text{Bx}} \leftrightarrow y_{\text{Bx}}: \rho = +0.576$, $x_{\text{DRE}} \leftrightarrow y_{\text{DRE}}: \rho = +0.446$). Formulated the 23D joint space $\mathbf{z} = [\mathbf{x}, |\boldsymbol{\Phi}|, \boldsymbol{\Phi}_{\text{coalesced}}] \in \mathbb{R}^{23}$ and regularized ensembles (Random Forests and ExtraTrees with $\text{max\_features} = \sqrt{23}$) to handle collinearity and small-sample constraints ($N=73$).
- **Consensus:** Formulated and executed `exp_9` under MCCV-30 in `tmux:0`. Condition $C_4$ (Joint ExtraTrees 23D) demonstrated massive, statistically ultra-significant gains over pure SHAP ($p = 1.86 \times 10^{-9}$), reaching $\overline{\text{Macro-F1}} = 0.2934 \pm 0.0224$, $S_{\text{weights}} = 0.8278 \pm 0.0119$, and record clinical rank alignments ($\rho(\text{PIRADS}) = +0.4486$, $\rho(\text{Bx}) = +0.4768$, $\rho(\text{DRE}) = +0.3547$, $\rho(\text{PSA}) = +0.1935$). Ratified $C_4$ as the definitive Subtask 1.3 champion.
