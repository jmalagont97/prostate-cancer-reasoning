### [2026-08-18] - Particle-Set Uncertainty Decomposition for the Tabular KDM (exp_24)
- **Principal Investigator Proposal (User):** A `KDMLayer`'s output density matrix is, structurally, a weighted particle set `{(w_j(x), p_j)}` — `dm2discrete` collapses it to `Σ_j w_j p_j` before `exp_23` ever sees it. Extract the full particle set (via `dm2comp`) and test whether its internal structure predicts the urologist's 3-class `confidence` annotation better than `exp_23`'s two collapsed scalars (`entropy`, `log_marginal`), including a Jensen's-inequality aleatoric/epistemic decomposition of predictive entropy (`H(Σw_jp_j) = Σw_jH(p_j) + I`) standard in Bayesian-ensemble uncertainty (BALD-style), and new particle weight-geometry signals (`h_weights`, `log_ess`, `w_max`) that depend only on the kernel weighting, not on the learned label content.
- **Co-Investigator Analysis (Gemini under active role):**
  1. *A structural degeneracy was identified before any run, not discovered after*: for a one-hot amplitude vector `u = e_1`, the Born-rule Jacobian `∂p_i/∂v_k = 2u_i(δ_ik − u_iu_k)/‖v‖` vanishes identically — a one-hot `c_y` (`exp_23`'s hard arm) is an exact gradient fixed point regardless of `y_train`. Confirmed three independent ways: analytically; against `exp_23`'s own grid (16/16 hard-arm `y_train=True` vs. `False` pairs bit-identical, 0/16 soft-arm pairs); and by a direct gradient probe (`max|grad c_y| = 0.000e+00` at one-hot init vs. `3.838e-03` at a smoothed init). This makes the textbook aleatoric/epistemic split trivially degenerate (`h_aleatoric ≡ 0`) on `exp_23`'s hard arm — not usable as originally proposed without modification.
  2. *Resolution — a third arm, not a fix to the existing two*: added Arm C, `c_y` initialized with ε-label-smoothed amplitudes (`ε∈{0.05,0.10,0.20}`, swept in a dedicated 18-config Phase A) and `y_train=True`, trained with the same hard binary-outcome loss as Arm A — the `confidence` annotation never enters training.
